GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

A few quick answers before you get started.

Is SkillSwap free?

SkillSwap is designed around free skill exchange. Members share knowledge rather than paying for lessons.

Do I need to be an expert to teach?

No. Share skills you are comfortable helping someone practise. Be honest about your experience.

How does an exchange work?

Start by finding a shared interest, then agree on a suitable topic, schedule, and way to learn together.

Is my information public?

The join form sends your submission to the site owner. Do not submit sensitive personal information. Any public member directory would need to be built separately.

Have a skill to share?

Someone out there may be hoping to learn exactly that.
